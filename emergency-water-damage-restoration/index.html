When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Mansfield

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
